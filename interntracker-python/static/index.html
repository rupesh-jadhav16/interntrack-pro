How it works

One platform. Four stakeholders. Zero chaos.

InternTracker digitises the entire internship lifecycle that colleges usually manage through spreadsheets and WhatsApp groups.

Students apply

Students complete profiles and apply to internships from verified companies with one click.

Companies hire

Companies post internships and move applicants through a clear pipeline — shortlist, interview, select.

Faculty track

Faculty review daily & weekly reports, catch at-risk students, and give feedback that actually counts.

T&P verifies

The T&P cell verifies companies and certificates, so every badge on the platform is earned.

Benefits

Built for every stakeholder

Each role gets its own dashboard, permissions, and view of the same source of truth.

For Students

One dashboard for applications, streaks, reports, deadlines, rewards, and certificates. Gamified progress keeps you consistent — and your rank motivates you.

For Colleges

Real-time visibility into every student's internship journey. Spot at-risk students before they fall behind, and showcase placement outcomes with analytics.

For Companies

Post roles, manage the applicant pipeline, and monitor your interns' daily progress with attendance and report approval — all in one place.

Verified Ecosystem

Only T&P-approved companies get the verified badge. Every certificate is scored for authenticity before it counts toward your record.

Rewards & Rankings

Points for reports, attendance, and completed internships. Live leaderboards make great work visible across the college.

Deadline Radar

Never miss an application window. Deadlines are grouped into overdue, due today, and upcoming — right on your dashboard.

Daily progress tracking

Every intern's day, on the record

Daily reports, weekly summaries, attendance check-in/out, mentor contact — students log it, faculty reviews it, companies see it.

  • Streak & points engine — +10 per approved daily report, +50 weekly, +5 per check-in.
  • Faculty review queue — approve or reject with feedback; students get notified instantly.
  • At-risk detection — low attendance, dead streaks, and report gaps surface automatically.
  • Internship tracker — on-campus, off-campus, college-provided, or self-found, with offer-letter upload.

Internship verification

No fake internships. No fake certificates.

The T&P cell reviews every company and every certificate before anything becomes official. Companies re-submit until they're verified; certificates get an authenticity score.

  • Verified company badge — students can trust every listing they apply to.
  • Authenticity scoring — certificates are scored and stored under unique codes.
  • Public verify lookup — anyone can check a certificate code from the student page.
